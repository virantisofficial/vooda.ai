# Vooda Trademark Policy

**Status: draft — subject to lawyer review before this repository is made public.**

The Vooda source code is licensed under the [Vooda Community Licence, Version 1.0](LICENSE.md). **The Vooda name and logo are not.** They are trademarks of Virantis, and the license explicitly says so:

> Except for displaying the License Details and identifying us as the origin of the Software, you have no right under these Terms and Conditions to use our trademarks, trade names, service marks or product names.

This document explains, in plain English, what that means in practice.

## Why this exists

Licenses are hard to enforce — you have to detect misuse inside infrastructure you cannot inspect, then litigate. Trademarks are easy to enforce, because the infringement is public by definition: anyone reselling Vooda has to *name* it to sell it.

So this policy is not bureaucracy. It is the practical mechanism that keeps someone from spinning up "Vooda Cloud" and selling your work back to you.

## The marks

| Mark | Covers |
|---|---|
| **VOODA** | the word, in any capitalisation |
| The Vooda logo | `logo/logo.svg`, `logo/logo-icon.svg`, and derivatives |

## What you may do — no permission needed

- **Say you use Vooda.** "We scan our repositories with Vooda." "Our security stack includes Vooda."
- **Describe compatibility**, using the mark as an adjective, never as your product's name: "compatible with Vooda", "a Vooda plugin", "built on Vooda", "Vooda integration for Acme".
- **Write about it** — blog posts, talks, tutorials, comparisons, reviews, criticism. Favourable or not.
- **Use the logo** to link to this repository or to vooda.ai, unmodified and with its aspect ratio intact.
- **Distribute unmodified Vooda** under the terms of the license, keeping the name.
- **Use the name in academic work**, with normal citation.

## What needs written permission

- Using "Vooda" (or anything confusingly similar) in **your product's name, company name, domain name, or app-store listing**.
- Using the logo in a way that **implies endorsement, partnership, certification, or affiliation**.
- **Merchandise** bearing the mark.
- Naming an event, meetup, or conference after Vooda.
- Registering "Vooda" or a confusing variant as a **trademark, domain, or social handle** in any jurisdiction.

Ask: **report@vooda.ai**

## What you may not do

- **Name a fork "Vooda"** or anything confusingly similar. Forking is permitted by the license; keeping the name is not. Pick your own name and say "a fork of Vooda" in your description — that's nominative use and it's fine.
- **Offer a commercial or hosted service under the Vooda name.** Note this is forbidden twice over: the licence forbids commercial and hosted-service use without a commercial licence, and this policy forbids the name.
- **Modify the logo** — recolour, redraw, add elements, or combine it with other marks.
- **Imply your modified version is official.** If you ship changes, say so clearly.
- Use the mark in a way that **disparages Vooda** or suggests we endorse something we don't.

## Forks — the short version

You are free to fork. You are not free to call it Vooda.

Good: *"Acmefork — a fork of Vooda with extra detectors."*
Not good: *"Vooda Community Plus"*, *"Vooda-NG"*, *"OpenVooda"*.

## Reporting misuse

**report@vooda.ai**. Include the URL and a screenshot if you can.
