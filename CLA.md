# Contributor License Agreement

**Status: draft — subject to lawyer review before this repository is made public.**

Thanks for wanting to contribute to Vooda. Before we can merge your first pull request, we need you to agree to this Contributor License Agreement. It's a one-time thing — sign once and every future PR is covered.

## Why we ask for this (the honest version)

Vooda is source-available under the [Vooda Community Licence, Version 1.0](LICENSE.md), and Virantis also offers Vooda commercially — hosted, supported, and with an enterprise agreement.

Here's the mechanics: **you own the copyright in code you write.** When you open a PR, your code arrives under the repository's license, which grants rights to *users*. It does not grant us the right to *sublicense* your code under different terms. Without that right, we could not include your contribution in the commercial edition — every accepted patch would become a permanent piece of the codebase we're not allowed to sell.

That's what this document fixes. It is a **license grant, not a copyright assignment**: you keep ownership of your work, and you can continue to use, relicense, and distribute your own contribution however you like. We just gain the right to ship it.

We're telling you this plainly because a CLA is a real ask, and you deserve to know exactly what it's for.

## How to sign

You don't need to do anything in advance. When you open your first pull request, a bot will comment with a link. Click through, sign in with GitHub, accept — takes about thirty seconds. The PR's CLA status check goes green and stays green for all your future contributions.

**Contributing on behalf of a company?** If your employer owns the IP in your work, we need a Corporate CLA signed by someone authorised to bind the company. Email **report@vooda.ai** and we'll sort it out.
